### Additional Device Channel safety correction — cross-connection disconnect

Device disconnect, like stale-lease cleanup, checks whether a **different still-live connection** exists for the same owner/device under the channel's transaction and owner/device advisory locks. It closes the requested connection's A/B grants, pending requests and remote sessions, but does not mark the device offline or cancel device queued jobs while another authorized connection remains active. Queued job cancellation is scoped to the exact **(device_id, owner_account_id)** pair; no action is ever authorized by unsigned account/node values.
