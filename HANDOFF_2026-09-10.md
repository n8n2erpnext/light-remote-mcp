## Linux signed updater closure

Structured Wall action `Run signed update now` is live. Backend/UI action opens a bounded maintenance session, submits the privileged updater start through the selected Linux leaf, and auto-closes the maintenance session after the job finishes. The reusable proof is `deploy/scripts/live-v09-maintenance-update-trigger.mjs`.

The AMD package layout is now root-owned and versioned under `/opt/gpt-operator-agent/releases`; `/opt/gpt-operator-agent/current` selects the active release. The updater timer is enabled/active and its acceptance manifest/signature URLs are configured to the temporary NetBird-only acceptance channel, not GitHub Releases.

Two real updater defects were found and fixed before release:
- ESM main detection failed when systemd invoked the updater through the `current` symlink. `invokedAsMain()` now compares canonical real paths; regression executes the updater through a symlink.
- post-restart validation could accept a crash-looping release during a transient `systemctl is-active` window. The updater now requires `ActiveState=active`, `SubState=running`, non-zero MainPID, stable for the health window; rollback must also reach stable health.

Good-path live proof: signed `0.9.0-rc.1` moved AMD from `0.9.0-dev` to `rc.1`; journal recorded `updated:0.9.0-dev->0.9.0-rc.1`, `current` pointed to the rc.1 release, and the agent returned `active/running` with a real MainPID.

Rollback live proof: signed/hash-valid `0.9.0-rc.2` intentionally shipped a non-executable bundled Node runtime. Updater failed visibly with `update_failed:update_rollback:0.9.0-rc.2`, restored `current` to `rc.1`, and recovered the agent to stable `active/running`. Restoring signed rc.1 then produced `up_to_date:0.9.0-rc.1` and updater success.
