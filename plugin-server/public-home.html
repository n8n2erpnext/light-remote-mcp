GET STARTED · 4 STEPS

From your first download to your first AI command.

Device enrollment and ChatGPT approval are separate. Complete both before asking the agent to work on your machine.

01

Create your account

Register or sign in to Light Remote. Your free account is the home for devices you own.

Create a free account →
02

Install & add your computer

Download the Windows, macOS or Linux client. Use Add a device in your dashboard and approve its enrollment code.

Choose your installer →
03

Authorize ChatGPT locally

Open the official plugin and sign in. On your computer's Local Wall, copy its A code, give it to ChatGPT, then enter the returned B code on the same Wall and approve.

Open ChatGPT plugin →
04

Try your first command

Once ChatGPT reports ready, ask: “Use Light Remote to check the system information on my computer.” You choose the target device.

Check setup progress →

Seeing Online in your account means the device reached the Hub. ChatGPT access starts only after its separate A/B approval. A code and B code are one-time, device-specific codes.

TRUST BOUNDARY

Remote does not mean unrestricted.

OAuth authenticates the account. A/B authorizes the exact MCP client to the exact device. Device-local policy still decides what can run.
